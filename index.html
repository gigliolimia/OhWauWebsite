    FAQ

    Have a question? We’re here to help.

    Do you come to my home?

    Yes. Oh Wau, Patitas! is at-home pet care in St. Lucie County and nearby.

    What’s included in a visit?

    Bath & Blow-Out, breed-specific haircuts, and add-ons like nails, ears, teeth, and a berry facial. Pick what you need when you book.

    Is grooming okay for anxious pets?

    Home visits are often calmer. Tell us about temperament in the notes and we’ll go gently.

    Do I need to agree to anything before booking?

    Yes. There’s a short grooming disclosure to check before a request is sent.
